# Tailored Solutions - Quick Start Guide

## Getting Started in 5 Minutes

---

## Step 1: Start the Website

Open Terminal (Mac) or Command Prompt (Windows) and run:

```bash
cd /path/to/home-improvements-business
npm run dev
```

Wait for: **"Ready in X.Xs"**

---

## Step 2: Open Your Browser

Go to: **http://localhost:3001**

---

## Step 3: Access Admin Panel

Go to: **http://localhost:3001/admin/cms**

---

## Admin Panel Quick Guide

### Edit Business Info
1. Click **"Business Info"** tab
2. Update your details
3. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Edit Statistics
1. Click **"Statistics"** tab
2. Update numbers
3. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Add Testimonials
1. Click **"Testimonials"** tab
2. Click **"+ Add Testimonial"**
3. Fill in details
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Add Portfolio Projects
1. Click **"Portfolio"** tab
2. Click **"+ Add Project"**
3. Fill in details
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

---

## Calendar Management

Go to: **http://localhost:3001/admin/calendar**

- Click **"Add Slot"** to add availability
- Click the trash icon to remove slots
- Navigate weeks using arrows

---

## WhisperAI Demo

Go to: **http://localhost:3001/admin/test-transcription**

1. Click **"Start Demo Consultation"**
2. Watch the pipeline process
3. View the generated document
4. Click **"Edit & Customize Quotation"**
5. Adjust pricing as needed
6. Click **"Send to Client"**
